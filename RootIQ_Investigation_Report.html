4. Specialist Findings

Sales Specialist

  • F1: Entire revenue decline (¥475,265 from 3 Supplier A SKUs) = 106.2% of total decline. Other SKUs offset with ¥27,916 gains.
  • F2: Volume decline is dominant driver (~70-75%), not pricing (0%) or mix (~25-30%). No price changes or promotions active.
  • F3: Retail stores hit harder (-41.1% avg) than online (-33.6% avg) — 7.5pp gap suggesting inventory allocation prioritized online.
  • F4: Regional decline near-uniform (34.5%-38.1%, 3.6pp spread) — supply-side cause, not localized demand.
  • F5: Substitution recovered only ¥27,916 (6.2% of loss) — limited product overlap, weak cross-sell.

Assessment: Not a sales execution problem. A supply-side disruption exposing structural vulnerability: over-concentration in a single supplier without sales contingency.

Inventory & Procurement Specialist

  • F1: Single-supplier dependency: 54% revenue from Supplier A, sole-source exclusive, no backup. ¥475,265 revenue loss (-66.8%).
  • F2: Supplier A collapsed: OTD 94.2%→12.5%, fulfillment 100%→9.2%, lead time 7d→28d.
  • F3: Safety stock (250-300 units / 13-15 days) consumed within 5-6 days — grossly insufficient for 54% of revenue.
  • F4: No backup activation despite 16-day warning window. 10 documented events, zero procurement actions before Oct 15.
  • F5: Suppliers B & C demonstrated resilience (93-99% fulfillment, +3.6% to +6.7% growth) — validates diversification.

Assessment: Structural over-reliance on a single sole-source supplier with no backup. Production failure was the trigger; concentration was the vulnerability.

Customer & Operations Specialist

  • F1: Churn surged +368.9% (145→680), beginning Oct 5 — directly mirroring stockout timeline.
  • F2: Complaints exploded +1,014.3% (28→312), 78.5% "Product Out of Stock."
  • F3: AOV declined -9.0% (¥412.4→¥375.2) due to loss of higher-priced SKUs — ~29% of revenue loss.
  • F4: New customer acquisition dropped -28.2% (620→445) — stockouts reduced visibility and conversion.
  • F5: Fulfillment time increased +81.0% (2.1→3.8 days) for available SKUs due to demand surge.

Assessment: Single upstream supply chain failure cascaded through inventory into customer behavior, order economics, and operational capacity. The 640 lost repeat customers represent future revenue extending well beyond October.

Specialist Convergence

All three specialists independently converged on the same root cause: sole-source supplier concentration risk combined with Supplier A's production failure and absence of backup sourcing. This multi-domain convergence significantly increases confidence.
